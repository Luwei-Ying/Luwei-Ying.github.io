      <p class="eyebrow">Book manuscript</p>
      <h2 id="book-title"><cite>The Logic of Terrorist Propaganda</cite></h2>
      <p class="book-lede">What do militant movements actually say—and which appeals move people to act?</p>
      <p>This book compares how movements across ideologies communicate. It distinguishes between <em>doctrine</em>, the order a movement says it wants to build, and <em>desire</em>, the belonging, honor, and status it offers potential recruits.</p>
      <p>The project brings scattered and short-lived material into a common frame, pairing public communications with internal records, social media, and interviews with civilians who lived under militant rule.</p>
